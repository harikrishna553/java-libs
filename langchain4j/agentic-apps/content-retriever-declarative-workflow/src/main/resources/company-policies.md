# 1. Annual Leave Policy

## Purpose

The Annual Leave Policy provides employees with paid time away from work for vacation, personal needs, and rest.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Employees are entitled to **24 days of annual leave per calendar year**.

Annual leave is credited according to the organization's leave management process.

Employees are encouraged to plan their leave in advance wherever possible.

## Leave Approval

Planned annual leave requires approval from the employee's reporting manager.

Employees should submit planned leave requests before making travel or other personal commitments.

Managers may consider the following when approving leave:

* Project commitments
* Customer deliverables
* Team availability
* Business-critical activities
* Existing leave requests from other team members

## Extended Leave

Employees planning to take more than **five consecutive working days** of annual leave should discuss the request with their manager in advance.

Extended leave may require additional planning to ensure that project responsibilities are appropriately covered.

## Unplanned Leave

Employees who are unable to attend work due to an unexpected situation should inform their manager as early as reasonably possible.

Where applicable, the employee should also update the organization's leave management system.

## Leave Balance

Employees are responsible for periodically checking their available leave balance.

Leave should normally be requested only when sufficient leave balance is available.

# 2. Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

The Remote Work Policy provides employees with flexibility to work outside the office while maintaining productivity, collaboration, security, and customer commitments.

## Remote Work Eligibility

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **two days per week**.

Remote work arrangements require approval from the employee's reporting manager.

The availability of remote work may depend on:

* Project requirements
* Customer expectations
* Team collaboration needs
* Security requirements
* Role-specific responsibilities

## Working Hours

Employees working remotely are expected to remain available during normal business hours.

Employees should attend scheduled:

* Team meetings
* Customer meetings
* Project discussions
* Reviews
* Mandatory organizational sessions

## Workplace Requirements

Employees working remotely should use an appropriate work environment that allows them to perform their duties effectively.

Employees are expected to have:

* Reliable internet connectivity
* A suitable workspace
* Access to required company systems
* Company-approved devices where applicable

## Information Security

Employees must follow the same information-security requirements while working remotely as they would while working from an office.

Company-confidential information must not be exposed to unauthorized individuals.

Employees must use approved security mechanisms such as:

* Corporate VPN
* Multi-factor authentication
* Approved company devices
* Secure networks

## Office Attendance

Managers may request employees to work from the office when required for:

* Customer meetings
* Workshops
* Team planning
* Project milestones
* Training
* Security-sensitive activities

# 3. Learning and Certification Policy

## Purpose

The Learning and Certification Policy encourages employees to continuously improve their professional and technical skills.

## Certification Reimbursement

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved professional certifications up to **INR 50,000 per calendar year**.

The reimbursement limit applies to eligible certifications completed during the applicable calendar year.

## Eligible Certifications

Examples of certifications that may qualify include:

* Cloud certifications
* Software testing certifications
* Artificial Intelligence certifications
* Cybersecurity certifications
* Project management certifications
* Agile certifications
* Data engineering certifications
* Architecture certifications

Eligibility depends on business relevance and manager approval.

## Pre-Approval

Employees should obtain approval before registering for a certification if they intend to request reimbursement.

The approval process may consider:

* Relevance to the employee's role
* Relevance to current projects
* Business value
* Learning objectives
* Certification cost

## Reimbursement Requirements

Employees requesting certification reimbursement should provide relevant supporting information, such as:

* Certification name
* Certification provider
* Proof of payment
* Proof of successful completion
* Manager approval

## Failed Certification Attempts

Certification reimbursement is normally applicable after successful completion unless a specific learning program explicitly permits reimbursement for unsuccessful attempts.

## Training Programs

Employees may also participate in organization-sponsored learning programs.

These programs may include:

* Instructor-led training
* Online learning
* Technical workshops
* Internal communities of practice
* Hands-on labs
* Certification preparation sessions

# 4. Business Travel Policy

## Purpose

The Business Travel Policy provides guidance for employees traveling for approved business purposes.

## Travel Approval

Business travel must be approved by the appropriate manager before bookings are made.

Employees should follow the organization's approved travel-booking process.

## Eligible Travel Expenses

Eligible business travel expenses may include:

* Airfare
* Train travel
* Local transportation
* Hotel accommodation
* Business meals
* Visa fees
* Other approved travel-related expenses

## Expense Claims

Employees should retain receipts and supporting documents required for reimbursement.

Expense claims should be submitted within **30 days after completion of the business trip**.

## Personal Expenses

Personal expenses incurred during business travel are not reimbursable unless explicitly approved under another company policy.

# 6. Information Security Policy

## Purpose

The Information Security Policy protects company, employee, and customer information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or loss.

## Password Security

Employees must use strong passwords and follow the organization's password-management requirements.

Passwords must not be:

* Shared with other employees
* Stored in source code
* Committed to source-control repositories
* Included in publicly accessible documents
* Shared through unsecured communication channels

## Secrets Management

Sensitive credentials such as:

* Database passwords
* API keys
* Access tokens
* Private keys
* Service-account credentials

must be stored using approved secrets-management solutions.

## Company Devices

Employees should use company-approved devices when accessing restricted company information.

Devices should have:

* Required security patches
* Endpoint protection
* Device encryption where applicable
* Approved security software

## Security Incidents

Suspected security incidents should be reported promptly through the organization's approved incident-management process.

Examples include:

* Accidental credential exposure
* Suspicious login attempts
* Lost company devices
* Unauthorized data access
* Malware detection

# 8. Employee Referral Policy

## Purpose

The Employee Referral Policy encourages employees to recommend suitable candidates for open positions.

## Eligibility

Employees may refer candidates for eligible open roles through the organization's approved referral system.

## Referral Bonus

A referral bonus may be provided when:

* The candidate is hired for an eligible position
* The referral was submitted before the candidate entered the recruitment process
* All applicable referral-program conditions are satisfied

The exact referral amount may vary by role and hiring program.

## Payment

Referral payments, where applicable, are processed according to the conditions of the employee-referral program.
